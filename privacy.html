Antique Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Antique: AI Story Maker mobile application and its related services (the "App") collect, use and share information. The App is operated by NouSphere Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("NouSphere Labs", "we", "us" or "our"). If you have questions, contact us at info@nouspherelabs.com.

In short
  • We do not require an account. We do not ask for your email or phone number to use the App.
  • You do not have to use your own photo. You can pick one of the App's ready-made characters instead. Their faces are generated by AI and are not real people.
  • If you add a photo, the App asks you to confirm that it is your photo or that the person in it agreed. The photo is sent to our AI processing partners only to draw the scenes and animate the film you ask for (see section 3).
  • Your photos, stories, images, narration and films are stored on your device. We do not keep copies of them on our own servers.
  • We do not collect, keep or sell biometric identifiers, we do not identify you from your face, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • The App contains no analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

1. Information we process

Your hero: name and photo

To star in a movie, you create a hero by entering a name and choosing a photo with the system photo picker, or you pick a ready-made character. The App only receives the photo you select; it cannot see the rest of your photo library. Before saving a hero with your own photo, you confirm that it is your photo or that the person in it agreed to its use.

The App removes location and other metadata from the photo and stores it on your device. Each time a movie or storybook is created, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing partner fal.ai, which uses it as a reference so that the hero looks the same in every scene image. The scene images that contain the hero are then sent to the video model that animates them.

Your story choices

The world, role, mission, mood, ending and narrator you choose, any wishes you type for your story, and the hero's name are sent to Perplexity, which runs Google's Gemini language model to write the story outline and screenplay. The narration text is then sent to fal.ai to be read aloud. If you describe your own world idea, that text is sent to Perplexity in the same way. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your wishes.

Generated content

Scene images, video clips, background music, narration and the finished film are created by our AI processing partners and downloaded to your device. They are saved in the App's private storage, protected by iOS file protection and excluded from iCloud backups. You can delete a single film or hero with a long press, or everything at once under Settings > Privacy & storage. Deleting the App also removes them, unless you have saved them to your photo library.

Saving to your photo library

If you choose to save a film or an image, the App asks for add-only access to your photo library. With this permission the App can add items but cannot read or see your existing photos.

Purchases and credits

Credit packs are sold as in-app purchases and processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details. Your credit balance and the identifiers of the purchases already credited are stored on your device so that each purchase is added only once.

Diagnostics

The App does not include analytics, advertising or crash reporting tools of its own. If you have allowed it in iOS settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us under Apple's privacy policy.

Messages you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and we use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of your request.

2. How we use information

  • To create the movies and storybooks you request and deliver them to your device.
  • To sell and add credit packs.
  • To protect the App and its users from fraud and abuse.
  • To respond to your support and privacy requests and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Biometric information

To keep the hero recognizable, the AI models look at the face in the photo you choose while they create your scenes. This happens only while your movie is being made. We do not extract, collect, store or keep face geometry, face templates or any other biometric identifier, we do not use faces to identify or recognize anyone, and we never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric information.

The App asks you to confirm before a photo is used that it is your photo or that the person in it agreed. This section is intended to meet the requirements of laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act and the Washington biometric privacy law, to the extent they apply.

5. Data retention

  • Hero photos, stories, images, narration, music and films: stored on your device until you delete them or the App. Never stored on our own servers.
  • Copies held by our AI processing partners: kept for a limited period under the retention policies of fal.ai and Perplexity described in section 3, then deleted by them.
  • Credit balance and purchase records: stored on your device. Apple keeps its own purchase records.
  • Support and privacy requests: kept as long as needed to resolve your request and, for privacy requests, for 24 months as required by law.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent (using your photo for AI processing, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the hero), the performance of our contract with you (creating your movies and providing credits), and our legitimate interests (keeping the App secure and preventing abuse; you can object by contacting us). 8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or a higher age where required by local law). A photo of a child may only be used by the child's parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.
